# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

General customers who need documents printed and delivered or document originals attested and returned.

## Product Purpose

QuickDoc is a customer-facing marketplace for print-and-deliver and document services.

## Positioning

A multi-vendor marketplace that brings print-and-deliver and document-attestation services together in one customer experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The customer web app supports account registration, PDF upload, print-option quotation, order placement workflows, and customer account management.
- Attestation services are represented in the platform and customer app; availability depends on configured services.
- The platform does not determine legal eligibility for attestation, certify providers automatically, or invent country-specific compliance rules.
- The product roadmap tracks order completion, successful delivery, provider acceptance time, upload failures, payment reconciliation, and support resolution.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: QuickDoc.
- The existing QuickDoc logo in `apps/customer-web/public/logo/quickdoc-logo.svg` is the available brand asset.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product and release requirements: `docs/01-prd.md`.
- The workspace contains no verified testimonials, customer ratings, case studies, or performance benchmarks. Do not invent these.

## Product Principles

- Keep document-service requests understandable and actionable.
- Make the service options and next steps clear to customers.
- Preserve factual limits around provider verification and legal attestation eligibility.
- Do not imply service availability, performance, or customer outcomes without evidence.
