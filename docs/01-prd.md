# Product Requirements Document

## Goal
Provide a trusted, multi-vendor marketplace for Print & Deliver and Attest & Return services, beginning with a responsive customer web experience and a shared API.

## Release 1 scope
- Phone-based customer identity and profile.
- Secure PDF upload and print-option quotation.
- Nearby shop discovery and order placement.
- Payment abstraction with server-side confirmation.
- Shop fulfilment status and rider delivery tracking.
- Notifications, audit history and support escalation.

Attestation is modelled in the platform foundation but is not part of the first implementation slice. Native mobile apps, live maps and production payment providers are later phases.

## Success measures
Quote-to-order completion, successful document delivery, provider acceptance time, upload failure rate, payment reconciliation accuracy and support resolution time.

## Non-goals
The platform does not determine legal eligibility for attestation, certify providers automatically or invent country-specific compliance rules.
